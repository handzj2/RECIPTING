Verification

Anyone with the link or QR can check a receipt. No HandzJ account is required.

Statuses you may see

✓ VALID RECEIPT
The receipt record exists and is currently valid on the HandzJ platform.
✕ RECEIPT VOIDED
The receipt exists but was voided by the merchant. It is no longer valid.
? RECEIPT NOT FOUND
No matching record was found for the number or identifier provided.
! VERIFICATION UNAVAILABLE
The verification service could not complete the check (for example a temporary connection problem). Try again later.

What verification means

HandzJ verification confirms the receipt record maintained by HandzJ.

It does not automatically confirm:

  • government certification
  • EFRIS or URA tax status
  • merchant tax registration
  • legality of the underlying transaction

We do not use phrases such as “URA verified” or “government verified”. See also Tax & EFRIS and the Disclaimer.

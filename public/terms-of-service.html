Terms of Service

These terms describe the HandzJ Digital Receipts service as operated today.

1. Service description

HandzJ Digital Receipts is a software service that lets merchants create, store, share, and allow online verification of commercial digital receipts. It is operated by HandzJ Tech Solutions.

2. Accounts

Merchants register with an email and password (or the authentication methods provided). You must provide accurate account information and keep login credentials secure. You are responsible for activity under your account.

3. Identity verification (KYC)

We may ask for legal name and National ID/NIN to verify merchant identity and reduce fraud. This information is private and is not printed on customer receipts or shown on the public verification page. KYC approval confirms identity only; it does not guarantee unlimited use. We may suspend accounts for misuse regardless of KYC status.

4. Merchant responsibilities

  • Ensure receipt content you enter is accurate.
  • Use the service only for legitimate business receipts.
  • Comply with applicable tax and e-invoicing laws (including EFRIS where required).
  • Not use the service for fraud, spam, or illegal activity.

4. Receipts and verification

Each issued receipt is registered under your merchant account with a receipt number and verification record. Online verification checks the HandzJ record. Voiding a receipt updates its status. Verification does not mean government or tax certification.

5. Trial, receipt packs, and subscription

New accounts may receive a 5-day free trial and, where enabled, a small number of free receipt credits (currently 5). After the trial ends, historical receipts remain accessible. Issuing new receipts requires one of:

  • remaining receipt pack credits, or
  • an active paid subscription.

Receipt packs are prepaid entitlements to issue a fixed number of digital receipts (for example 10 credits from UGX 5,000). One credit is consumed only when a receipt is successfully issued. Viewing, downloading, sharing, verifying, or voiding a receipt does not consume a credit. Credits do not expire on a monthly calendar; a long-stop inactivity period may apply as stated at purchase.

Subscription plans (currently UGX 50,000 per month or UGX 500,000 per year) allow continuous issuance while the subscription is active and do not consume pack credits during that period. Pack and subscription prices may be updated by HandzJ; the amount charged is the price confirmed at activation.

6. Payment and activation

Payment is arranged via Mobile Money (or other methods HandzJ accepts) and support contact. You must provide a valid payment reference and your login email. Activation is confirmed when HandzJ records the payment and either credits your account (packs) or sets an active subscription period. We do not currently operate an automatic card payment gateway. Failed, incomplete, or unmatched payments are not activated.

7. Acceptable use

You may not attempt to break security, access other tenants’ data, reverse-engineer the service for harm, or issue receipts that misrepresent payments.

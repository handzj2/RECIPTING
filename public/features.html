Features

What the product actually does today.

For the merchant

  • Issue receipts — customer, items, amount, payment method, permanent receipt number.
  • Receipt image — PNG of the same receipt shown on screen (for WhatsApp and download).
  • PDF and print — when you need a paper or PDF copy.
  • Receipt register — search and open past receipts in your account.
  • Void — mark a receipt voided; verification reflects that status.
  • Branding — business name, contacts, and logo on receipts.
  • Team access — on paid plans where configured.

For the customer

  • Keep the receipt image offline.
  • Scan the QR or open the link to see VALID, VOIDED, or NOT FOUND.
  • No login required for verification.

What it does not do

  • Does not replace URA EFRIS or other tax e-invoicing systems.
  • Does not certify government tax status.
  • Does not automatically send WhatsApp or email without the merchant completing the share flow.
  • Is not an ERP, POS, inventory, or accounting package.

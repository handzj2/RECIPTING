Digital receipts and EFRIS

Two different tools. Do not confuse them.

HandzJ digital receipt

A commercial, customer-facing electronic receipt: image, number, QR, and online check against the HandzJ record. Useful for proof of payment, WhatsApp sharing, and day-to-day operations.

EFRIS

EFRIS (Electronic Fiscal Receipting and Invoicing Solution) is the Uganda Revenue Authority system for official e-invoices and e-receipts used for tax purposes. It is operated under URA rules and related tax law.

Important
HandzJ Digital Receipts do not automatically replace EFRIS or other government tax / e-invoicing requirements.

Merchant responsibility

Businesses are responsible for determining which tax and e-invoicing rules apply to them (for example VAT registration and any mandatory EFRIS sectors). HandzJ does not provide tax or legal advice.

For official EFRIS information, use URA sources such as ura.go.ug/en/efris.

Thresholds, sectors, and penalties change over time. Always confirm current requirements with URA or a qualified adviser.

Privacy Policy

This policy describes data the service actually processes.

Who we are

HandzJ Tech Solutions operates HandzJ Digital Receipts (the “Service”).

Information we process

  • Account data — email and authentication data needed to sign in (via our auth provider).
  • Business profile — business name, contacts, logo, receipt prefix, and settings you enter.
  • Receipt data — customer name/phone/email you enter, items, amounts, payment method, references, status, and verification identifiers.
  • Technical data — information needed to run the site (for example session and browser storage for signed-in use).
  • Identity (KYC) — legal name and National ID/NIN you submit for review, stored privately for abuse prevention. NIN is never shown on receipts, QR codes, verification pages, or public search metadata.
  • Verification requests — when someone opens a verification link, the system looks up the receipt by number/identifier.

Why we process it

  • To provide receipt issuance, storage, sharing, and verification.
  • To manage trials and subscriptions.
  • To secure accounts and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support requests.

Infrastructure

The Service uses third-party infrastructure including hosting (e.g. Vercel) and database/authentication (e.g. Supabase). Those providers process data as needed to host and run the Service under their terms.

Access

Merchants access their own data. Platform operators may access data for support, security, and billing activation. Public verification shows limited receipt fields appropriate to verification — not a public directory of all transactions.

Retention

Receipt and account data are retained while the account is active and as needed for the Service. If you need deletion, contact support; legal or security retention may apply.

Your responsibilities

Only enter customer data you are entitled to process. Inform your customers as required by applicable law about how you use their details on receipts.

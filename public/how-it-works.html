How it works

A simple path from payment to a receipt your customer can keep and check online.

The workflow

Merchant creates receipt ↓ Receipt is registered in the merchant’s account ↓ Receipt number and verification record are preserved ↓ Digital receipt image is generated ↓ Merchant shares it (WhatsApp, download, email, or device share) ↓ Customer opens the image or scans the QR ↓ Verification page checks the HandzJ receipt record

What the merchant does

  1. Signs in and opens the receipt app.
  2. Enters customer, items, amount, and payment method.
  3. Issues the receipt — a permanent receipt number is assigned.
  4. Downloads the receipt image or shares it with the customer.
  5. Can later search the register, void a receipt if needed, or print/PDF.

What the customer gets

  • A digital receipt image they can save on their phone.
  • A verification link or QR code.
  • No HandzJ account required to verify.
  • No app install required.

No printer required

The core product is digital. Merchants can issue and share receipts without a thermal printer. Print and PDF remain available when a printer is useful.

WhatsApp is optional

WhatsApp is one way to share. Merchants can also download the image, use the device share sheet, copy the verification link, or open an email draft. The system does not claim a message was “sent” merely because WhatsApp or email was opened.
